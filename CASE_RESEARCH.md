# AFTERHOURS Case Research Notes

Updated 15 October 2026.

The cases NG-061, NG-062, US-061, and US-062 are original fictional scenarios. Their characters, records, timestamps, institutions, and events are invented. Public sources inform setting and research practice only; they are not evidence for the fictional events.

## Context sources
- NG-061 — The Last Shuttle: Lagos Metropolitan Area Transport Authority (LAMATA), official overview of integrated transport planning and operations.
- NG-062 — The Market Stall Ledger: Lagos State Ministry of Commerce, Cooperatives, Trade & Investment; Anchor University Entrepreneurship Development Centre.
- US-061 — The Duplicate Wire: U.S. National Archives, online research tools for finding public records.
- US-062 — The Archive Room: U.S. National Archives, research guidance on locating records and understanding their provenance.

## Design rules
1. Show ordinary transport, study, community, and small-business life alongside mystery.
2. A mismatch, shared device, or access log does not by itself identify a responsible person.
3. Reward primary-record checks, timeline reconciliation, and explicit uncertainty.
4. Label fiction clearly and never present invented evidence as historical fact.
5. Protect real people and avoid sensationalising victims.
